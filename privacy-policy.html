7. Children's Privacy

CalcPK does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so appropriate action can be considered.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information that is voluntarily provided to us. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
