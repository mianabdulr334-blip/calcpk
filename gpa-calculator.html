How is GPA Calculated?

GPA = Total Grade Points ÷ Total Credit Hours

Grade points are multiplied by the credit hours of each course and then divided by the total credit hours.

Note: Grade scales can vary between universities. Check your institution's official grading policy for exact GPA calculations.
