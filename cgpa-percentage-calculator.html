How is CGPA converted to Percentage?

A simple general conversion is:

Percentage = (CGPA ÷ Maximum CGPA) × 100

Example: 3.2 CGPA out of 4.0 = 80%.

Note: Universities and boards may use different official conversion formulas. Always check your institution's rules when an official percentage is required.
